**Status:** Unapproved. Do not copy into `src/server/assistant/TEAM_SOP.md` or publish as a task template yet.

**Primary result:** To be agreed with the Head.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-23 (Asia/Tashkent).
**Scope:** One repeatable webinar from the initial brief through live delivery and follow-up. Exact marketing purpose and format are still open.

## Product mechanics already agreed

- Webinar date is separate from the overall task deadline.
- Relative checkpoint dates use calendar days and Asia/Tashkent time. Each row may specify its own time; blank time defaults to 18:00.
- The default overall task deadline can be webinar date +1 day and may be changed at creation.
- If the webinar date changes, unfinished automatically scheduled checkpoints move with it. Finished or manually rescheduled checkpoints stay fixed.
- Checkpoints are optional progress markers and do not gate submitting a task for review.

These describe the current app, not the team's approval or quality policy.

## SOP decisions to make together

1. **Purpose:** Primary measurable result and what is counted as a qualified result.
2. **Accountability:** One overall owner; content, SMM, digital, operator, speaker, and Head roles only where needed.
3. **Start rule:** Minimum lead time and required brief before setting the event date.
4. **Preparation:** Topic, speaker, registration, landing/invite, promotion, technical setup, rehearsal, and backup owner. Remove any step that is not part of your real process.
5. **Live event:** Who hosts, who handles questions, who watches the recording/stream, and when escalation is needed.
6. **Follow-up:** Recording, attendee/lead handoff, publication, and post-event result review.
7. **Completion evidence:** Required links or numbers, approval point, and what to do when a dependency slips.

## Mapping after approval

Use a one-task template with checkpoints for work owned by one person. Create separate linked workflow tasks when a deliverable changes owner or needs its own approval. The AI may suggest task-specific checkpoints from this SOP, but adding them still requires an exact proposal and user confirmation. The measured webinar result is reviewed separately from whether its preparation tasks were checked off.
